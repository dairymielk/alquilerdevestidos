Our Story

The owner purchased two beautiful gowns for her own debut and her sister’s upcoming celebration. She felt it would be such a waste to simply sell them afterward, so she decided to offer them for rental instead — so these stunning gowns would never be forgotten. And now, the collection has grown with four more gowns added, helping the business earn faster and recover the investment used to buy them.
